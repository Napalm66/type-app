## Design Specificity Verdict

The diagnostics grid, Key Tell callout, serif-shape spectrum, font-accurate anatomy diagram, and an Identify quiz that walks genuine perceptual reasoning are domain-specific artifacts that couldn't be reskinned onto another content type. Historically-accurate font lineage (Coelacanth, Tinos, both correctly attributed) roots this in real scholarship, not decoration.

The browser-injected overlay, run against the live rendered DOM instead, is the real signal here and found 30-38 anti-patterns per view. Most are false positives against choices this session just deliberately documented in DESIGN.md — the cream palette, the ambient shadow, the small uppercase kicker labels, the intentional 3.5%-opacity paper texture, the clipped magnifier lens (it's a magnifying glass; clipping is the point). One finding is real and worth acting on: low-contrast — ink-faint (#8a8371) on parchment/parchment-raised measures 3.3:1-3.6:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 floor for text — and ink-faint is the color DESIGN.md assigns to nearly every label, tagline, and era note in the system, so this isn't an edge case, it's load-bearing. The LLM review's own accessibility pass (missing .filter-chip focus states) didn't catch this; the detector did.

**Visual overlays**: overlay injection succeeded and ran against three states (Explore grid, Timeline, detail modal); findings are summarized above and in Priority Issues below.

## Overall Impression

The system is coherent and genuinely earns its "Specimen Ledger" identity — this doesn't read as a template with type-classification content poured into it. The weak spot isn't the visual language, it's a handful of places where the interaction stops short of the product's own stated bar: the Identify quiz dead-ends into a text summary instead of the anatomy diagram and magnifier that are the actual differentiators, and the low-contrast tertiary text undercuts a product whose primary audience does graded, close-reading work.

## What's Working

- Live "try your own text" specimen: typing updates all 13 cards instantly in period-accurate lineages.
- The anatomy magnifier's touch handling: tap-open, drag-reposition (offset-preserving, no snap), tap-close, close-on-exit-bounds — matches DESIGN.md's own spec exactly.
- Compare's diagnostics-grid-per-column layout: turns three classifications into a literal side-by-side Contrast/Axis/Serif table.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Tertiary text fails WCAG AA contrast sitewide**
Why it matters: ink-faint (#8a8371) on parchment measures 3.3:1-3.6:1 against a 4.5:1 requirement, assigned to labels/taglines/era text/diagnostic tags throughout.
Fix: Darken --ink-faint (and dark-mode counterpart) to clear 4.5:1, or restrict its use to decorative text and promote functional labels to --ink-soft.
Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P1] Identify quiz result is a dead end into the app's richest content**
Why it matters: renderResult() in js/identify.js shows only name/specimen/trail/Key Tell/description — no path to the anatomy diagram, magnifier, or serif spectrum.
Fix: Add a "View full diagnostic ->" action on the result that opens the same detail modal used from Explore/Timeline.
Suggested command: /impeccable onboard or /impeccable layout

**[P1] Filter chips have no visible keyboard focus state**
Why it matters: .tab, .specimen-tool-input, .spec-card, .quiz-option all define :focus-visible; .filter-chip never does.
Fix: Add .filter-chip:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--focus-ring); outline-offset: 2px; }
Suggested command: /impeccable audit

**[P2] Serif-shape spectrum's zoom is desktop-only despite promising itself on mobile**
Why it matters: attachSerifSpectrumZoom() wires only mouseenter/mouseleave; hint text renders unconditionally including on mobile.
Fix: Extend the magnifier's tap/drag pattern to the serif-spectrum row, or swap hint copy based on touch-capability check.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Compare's 3-item cap fails silently**
Why it matters: Clicking a 4th classification when 3 are selected is a confirmed no-op with no feedback.
Fix: Disable unselected chips at the cap with a tooltip, or show a brief inline message.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify
