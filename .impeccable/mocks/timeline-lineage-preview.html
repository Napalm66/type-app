Timeline — lineage tree (concept, with era bands + era photos)

Same era coverage as the current Gantt-style Timeline (Romanesque through Digital & Variable Age), redrawn as a lineage tree instead of a bar chart. Era bands run the full height and always take an equal share of the width, regardless of how long each era actually lasted — a clean, uniform layout where every label fits on one line and no era visually dominates the others. Each era band is filled with equal-width vertical strips that fade to transparent toward the base — dissolving into the era's own color rather than ending on a hard edge — where real black-and-white era photos drop in once supplied; wider eras just get more strips, always equal width within that era. Classification node names never wrap — each node's box extends past its natural date-driven width to fit its full name, pushing later nodes in the same lane over to make room.

Solid border — has lineage connections Dashed — standalone, no documented link Vertical strips, fading at the base — placeholder for real B&W era photos
