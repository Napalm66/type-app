Era photo placement — three options

Option 1 — small cluster tucked inside each band

1-3 square-cropped photos (count follows band width) stacked with a slight offset in the lower half of each band, leaving the era name/date clear at top. No shadows — separation between overlapping tiles comes from the 1px ink border alone. Closest to the current "one watermark per band" approach, just multiple smaller fixed-size photos instead of one stretched one — so nothing has to deform as bands resize on open/close.

Option 2 — thin spine, photos staggered above/below

The era band shrinks to a short labeled ribbon; photos live outside it, anchored by a thin dashed connector + small accent dot (reusing the lineage tree's own connector-dot motif) to a specific point along the ribbon, alternating above/below like the reference. Reads more like a museum wall timeline. Needs real extra vertical space in the layout — a bigger structural change than Option 1.

Option 3 — quiet at rest, reveals a cluster only when opened

Default view (Baroque, Classicism) stays completely clean — name and date only, no photos at all. The one "opened" era (Gothic, simulating a click) reveals 3 photos in the same cluster style as Option 1, inside its now-expanded band. Rewards the click-to-open interaction we already built with genuinely new content, rather than showing every photo all the time.
