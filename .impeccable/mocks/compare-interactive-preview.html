Compare — Two Interactive Directions

Two candidate ways to make comparing exactly two classifications more interactive than reading two columns of text side by side. Pick classifications below — both sections update together.

Direction 1

Specimen Overlay / Diff Slider

The same letter, same size, same baseline — drag to wipe between the two classifications' actual specimen fonts. Structural differences (contrast, serif weight, stress) are seen directly on the glyph instead of read as a label.

Direction 2

Diagnostic Radar

Contrast and stress-axis angle are real, already-tracked values (stress in actual degrees, same numbers the anatomy diagram uses); serif-bracket strength is an ordinal reading of the existing serif-structure category. Two overlaid shapes make where two classifications actually diverge visible at a glance, instead of three rows of adjectives to compare word-by-word.

Preview only — not wired into the real app. Fonts loaded from Google Fonts for this mock; the real app already self-hosts/loads these per classification. Radar axes: Contrast (none→high, 0–3), Stress (0°–34°, from the same AXIS_ANGLES used by the anatomy diagram), Serif Bracket (an ordinal reading of the serif-structure category, 0–3).
