## Scope

Full visual redesign, all four views (Explore, Identify, Timeline, Compare) plus the detail modal. Operate mode.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The grid itself is the proof of measurement — every classification is placed on a visible dashed-rule structure, not just described in prose. Refuses the category-default "cozy field-guide" arrangement (warm parchment, soft serif display, ambient shadows, hidden grid) this kind of reference app always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white ground (#fdfcfa panels on #efece6 page), near-black ink (#1c1a15). Thin dashed grid rules (vertical, occasional horizontal) with small circular end-caps, always visible as literal structure. Anton (condensed, heavy, all-caps) carries every name and headline at large scale. JetBrains Mono for indices, codes, era dates, and dimension/measurement labels. Inter for body copy and rotated section labels. Sealing-wax red (#a8391f) kept as the only color note, reserved for branch tags and measurement callouts — near-monochrome otherwise.

STORY: A student lands and immediately reads this as a precision instrument, not a blog — the visible grid and leader-line annotations prove every claim is measured. They scan a numbered list of huge classification names, land on one, and read its anatomy as dimensioned data (cap line/mean line/baseline/stress angle), trusting the answer because it's presented like a technical drawing.

FIRST VIEWPOINT: Brand lockup (two-line huge Anton "TYPE / CLASSIFIED") top-left pinned to the grid, tagline top-right. Rotated vertical labels (EXPLORE/IDENTIFY/TIMELINE/COMPARE, "13 CLASSIFICATIONS", era range) ride the dashed grid lines as literal navigation. Below: numbered entry rows, each a full-width huge Anton classification name with mono era date and a small colored branch tag at the trailing edge — this replaces the card-grid Explore view with a measured list. The anatomy panel extends the same dashed leader-line language already used in the app's diagram to the whole page.

FORM: User-pinned reference (a personal portfolio site with massive stacked client names on black, and the Cooper Hewitt "Type Culture Now" poster with dashed grid rules and rotated labels), explicitly requested translated to a white ground. Pinned decision — supersedes the earlier concept-seed roll (assigned: Library Card Catalog; winning challenger: Acetate Tab-Board Manual) per "a user- or brief-pinned decision beats the roll, always." Confirmed by user: "look and feel is right on spot with good typography."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
